06 · What changed this weekend

Your six indicators talked only to each other, through one shared board inside NinjaTrader. Big Money — the separate indicator ChatGPT "Astra" wrote — ran on its own chart in its own workspace, with its own window and no voice, and none of your cards knew it existed. That is why "the indicator by ChatGPT does not show up": it was never on your live workspace's charts.

Now (on GitHub; installs on your word)

Big Money writes one small file every time its read changes (and a heartbeat every five seconds). Your cards read that file and nothing else — no shared code, no shared memory, no second voice:

  • Lane ⑤ BIG MONEY on the SWEEPS card: headline · quality · reason, coloured green / coral / amber / grey; "quiet since 10:14" when the file goes stale; the exact data-state word when Big Money reports degraded data.
  • One row in the info box on every chart of the same instrument and contract (the 5 and the 15 included).
  • ASK: "what does Big Money say" → "Study status. Big Money says flow favors long, quality med, 1 second ago. …" — or "has been quiet for 35 minutes … not current" — or "unavailable".

A September file never shows on a December chart; a stale file is never spoken; a file that fails its contract is thrown away whole. Nothing about Big Money can move a stop, a size, a gate, a door or a rail.

08 · The words

Sweep
Price pokes through a real level (the six session levels: PDH, PDL, ONH, ONL, ORH, ORL) and closes back inside. The reclaim is the trade; the poke is the bait.
Grade A / B / C
A = the reclaim on the first candle at one of the six session levels. B and C are later candles or lesser levels: watch only.
Stage 0 / 1 / 2
What a lane may say. 0 = numbers only. 1 = an action word for grade A at half size (the sweep lane, since your yes of 2026-09-07). 2 = full size, only when the Sunday scoreboard says PROMOTABLE and you say so.
Flag
A strong pole, a shallow rest that keeps over half the pole, then a 2-minute close through the flag line. FLAG 1, 2, 3 · final counts them.
The W and the M
A double bottom or double top. Its one door: the first 2-minute close through the neckline. Nothing is priced or spoken before that close.
Neckline / shelf
The shelf is the two tests' price; the neckline is the high between them (for a W) that the close must beat.
Wall
A strong level in the trade's path: the six session levels, the live 5- and 15-minute swings, your own lines. The first target sits two ticks in front of the first wall.
Tide
THE ANSWER's word. With the tide = full rules; against it = half size, first target only, and only with a wall behind you.
Delta
Buy volume minus sell volume on the tape. Lane ④ names who is in control every candle; the voice speaks only when control changes hands.
Flow read
Big Money's word: FLOW FAVORS LONG / SHORT, LONG / SHORT FLOW ONLY, DO NOT CHASE UP / DOWN, or one of its WAIT words. MED is its highest quality — it never claims HIGH.
The first two signals
The first two qualifying signals of a session are never taken: numbers and a lesson only.
The rails
The Day Guard's rules on the live account: the 09:45 start, the −$600 line (flat only), the 15-minute loss clock, the 30-minute cooldown, the three-loss lock, no entries after hours.
Numbers only / Your call / No trade
The three endings a priced sentence can have. Only "Your call." ever comes with an action word.
