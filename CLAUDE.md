# final-edge-playbook — project memory

**What this is:** a one-page teaching app for Arnie: what the Final Edge NinjaTrader cards say and what he does — the six cards (+ Big Money's window), the SWEEPS card's five lanes, the voice's sentences, the Day Guard's rails with the measured numbers behind each, an example morning as a clickable stepper, a daily checklist, and the words. Built 2026-09-12 (evening) on his ask "make me an app that demonstrates very easily what to do, what I'll be watching for, and how I can be much more successful if I follow what it says."

**Source of truth for its CONTENT:** the indicator repo `final-edge-nt8-indicator` — `docs/LEGEND.md`, the SWEEPS teaching packs, SESSION_NOTES, the CLAUDE.md laws (the M2K risk canon, THE LIVE RAILS), REVIEWS. Every sentence quoted here was transcribed from those files as they stood on 2026-09-12 (SWEEPS v3.38.0 · LEVELS v10.38.0 · GATES v5.76.0 · DAY GUARD v1.9.0 · SWINGS v5.54 · BIG MONEY 2.1.1-study). **When a card changes, this page changes** — the same standing order as the teaching pack (Arnie, 2026-08-11). Prices in the walkthrough are EXAMPLES and are labelled so; the measured numbers (the 15-month ledger, the hour scoreboard, the studies) are real and cite their source line.

**Laws:** never a fabricated statistic or a promise of profit (his rule 3; the "honest line" block says exactly what is and is not proven) · no action word is ever presented as this page's advice — it quotes the suite's sentences and says what they mean · trading design stack (Oxanium + Space Mono, amber/emerald/coral, the cards' own navy #0B1424) — trading only · the Narrator is `narrator.js` copied from trend-check-pro (storage keys `fep_tts_*`), never rebuilt · PWA files present (manifest.json, icon.svg, the meta tags) · Created/Revised dates in the sidebar and the footer, bump on every change.

**Deploy:** GitHub `arnoldshapiro-del/final-edge-playbook` (main) → Netlify auto-build → https://final-edge-playbook.netlify.app (static, publish `.`, no build command). Update = edit → commit → push; never the Netlify CLI.
